# Blog Voice Guide: the "Strategist-Practitioner" voice

**Source:** DSM Central (Session 222, 2026-06-15), inbox entry `2026-06-15_dsm-central-blog-voice.md`.
**Status:** Standing voice guide. Default voice for the author's blog posts and reader-facing prose.
**Calibration pairing:** read alongside `content/about-me.md` and `content/about.md` at session start. This guide is the prescriptive spec; the About pages are the lived idiolect.

## Why

The author confirmed a preferred writing voice for his blog and external prose after several iterations converged on it. Use it as the default so output stays aligned without re-deriving it each time. It blends a senior AI strategist consultant (his CV persona) with his Take AI Bite About-Me idiolect (https://take-ai-bite.com/about-me/).

## The voice (reproducible spec)

- **Persona / stance:** First-person operator who designs human-AI systems and stays in control of them. "Mechanical engineer by training, data scientist by career, system thinker by nature." Confident, no hype.
- **Tone:** Measured, professional, intellectually rigorous, but warm and personal. Framed as a consultant assessing (decision plus evidence), not narrative storytelling.
- **Rhythm:** Short declarative punches ("Nothing ships without my judgment.") interleaved with longer flowing sentences carrying embedded clauses. Occasional philosophical aside. Contrast structures ("not X but Y"; "The brief was not 'improve this'. It was...").
- **Vocabulary:** systems, judgment, discipline, understanding, position, evaluator, signal. Technical-philosophical hybrid, plain and precise, no buzzword inflation.
- **Signature phrases (his own, reuse in his posts):** "the human stays in the loop on purpose", "without becoming a rubber stamp", "nothing ships without my judgment", "AI amplifies, it does not replace", comprehension before shipping.
- **Mechanics:** comma for connecting phrases, never em dash; never space-comma-space.
- **Do:** open on a strategic observation; take a position early; let the principle surface in his own words; one well-placed informal pivot allowed ("a structured roast") amid professional prose.
- **Don't:** narrative scene-setting; hype; em dashes; AI-tell patterns (rule-of-three inflation, "it's not just X, it's Y" overuse).
