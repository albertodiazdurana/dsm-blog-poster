## Problem statement

The PM blog post establishes that all ten PMP knowledge areas are running inside DSM/TAB. Section 4 names a second layer: the organizational function that owns the methodology across projects, the PMO. That observation opens a dedicated story about what it means to run a PMO where the team members are AI agents, compliance enforcement runs on every model turn, and the organization lives across a multi-repository ecosystem rather than inside a company. The research exists; the post does not.

## Story angle

"I built a PMO and didn't call it that."

The hook mirrors the PM blog's discovery narrative: a structure was running before it had a name. The parallel holds for every canonical PMO function, standards authorship, template governance, tooling deployment, compliance enforcement, change management adoption, coaching, strategic roadmap, and the hub-spoke CoE model. The single genuine extension: AI agents as team members require enforcement at the turn level, not the audit level. The PMO framing holds everywhere except at the place where it had to be extended.

## Candidate structure

1. **Hook:** The inbox was Communication Management. The whole system is a PMO.
2. **What a PMO is (minimal):** authoritative source of standards, templates, tooling, governance. Not a project team. The function that defines how project work happens everywhere else.
3. **Six functions, all running:** standards authorship, template governance, tooling deployment, compliance enforcement, change management adoption, strategic roadmap. Each one mapped concretely to a DSM artifact.
4. **The police/coach tension:** hooks are the police (occurrence enforcement), methodology documents are the coach. DSM's resolution is the CoE model: hooks enforce the baseline, but the value comes from the collaborative protocols.
5. **The single extension:** AI agents as team members. Compliance enforcement at the turn level, not the audit level. The one place the PMO framing had to be extended rather than reused.
6. **What the role is called:** PMO Director of an AI-agent organization. The "develops" in "develops and maintains" carries more weight here than in most PMO contexts.
