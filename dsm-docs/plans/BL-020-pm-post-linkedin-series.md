# BL-020: LinkedIn series for the PM blog post

**Status:** Open
**Priority:** Medium
**Date Created:** 2026-05-12 (Session 22)
**Source:** `content/blog/2026-04-10-project-management-for-the-agentic-stakeholder/index.md`
**Origin:** The PM post was published S21 with no LinkedIn cross-post. User has been silent on LinkedIn since 2026-04-09 (last published post, "Think Ahead"). This BL drafts the LinkedIn fragments that distribute the PM post across multiple short posts over the following days.

## Problem statement

The PM blog post is dense (about 2,200 words, eight sections, two narrative arcs: the recognition story and the stakeholder extension). One LinkedIn post cannot carry it. A six-fragment series turns the post into a sequence the user can publish every one to three days, each fragment standing on its own while pointing back to the full post on the blog.

The series also primes the audience for BL-019 (the PMO Director post), which closes the project-management arc. Fragment 6 is the handoff: it teases the PMO observation that the PM post named in Section 4 and that BL-019 picks up.

## Story angle

Six short narratives, each anchored on one moment of recognition from the PM post:
1. The inbox was a Communication Management Plan.
2. Ten knowledge areas, all running, none named.
3. I built the PM chapter before I knew it was a framework.
4. The stakeholder the PMP curriculum did not have.
5. PMI is in the AI conversation. This is a third direction.
6. There is also an organization (handoff to BL-019).

Each fragment is independently readable and ends with a link to the full blog post. The series is sequenced so each fragment plants a thread the next one picks up, but does not require the previous one to make sense.

3. Each published fragment appended to `dsm-docs/blog/linkedin-posts.md` with URL and "Status: Published" entry, matching the established pattern.

## Success criteria

1. Six fragments drafted, each 250 to 400 words, plain-text, first-person, ending with hashtags and a "Full post:" link.
2. Each fragment passes the /humanizer gate before publication.
3. Each fragment, on its own, gives a reader something to take away without requiring the blog post.
4. Fragment 6 ends with a clean handoff to the PMO post (BL-019), so the series and the closing post read as one arc.
5. All six fragments published to LinkedIn and the URLs recorded.

## Workflow per fragment

1. Agent drafts into the fragments file (Status: Draft).
2. User reviews in place, edits or redirects.
3. Agent runs /humanizer (Status: Humanized).
4. User reviews again, copies to LinkedIn, posts.
5. User shares the URL; agent moves status to Published with URL, and appends a Post entry to `dsm-docs/blog/linkedin-posts.md`.

Fragments are drafted one at a time with per-fragment approval, per the chunked-drafting protocol (DSM_0.2 §8.10).

## Closing

BL-020 closes when all six fragments are Published. BL-019 (PMO Director blog post) then carries the project-management arc forward as a full blog post plus its own LinkedIn cross-post.
