# Blog Voice Guide: the "Strategist-Practitioner" voice

**From:** DSM Central (Session 222, 2026-06-15)
**To:** blog-poster
**Type:** Standing voice guide (apply to the author's blog posts going forward)

## Why

The author confirmed a preferred writing voice for his blog / external prose after
several iterations converged on it. Use this as the default voice for his posts so
output stays aligned without re-deriving it each time. It is a blend of a senior AI
strategist consultant (his CV persona) and his Take-AI-Bite About-Me idiolect
(https://take-ai-bite.com/about-me/).

## The voice (reproducible spec)

- **Persona / stance:** First-person operator who designs human-AI systems and stays
  in control of them. "Mechanical engineer by training, data scientist by career,
  system thinker by nature." Confident, no hype.
- **Tone:** Measured, professional, intellectually rigorous, but warm and personal.
  Framed as a consultant assessing (decision + evidence), not narrative storytelling.
- **Rhythm:** Short declarative punches ("Nothing ships without my judgment.")
  interleaved with longer flowing sentences carrying embedded clauses. Occasional
  philosophical aside. Contrast structures ("not X but Y"; "The brief was not
  'improve this'. It was…").
- **Vocabulary:** systems, judgment, discipline, understanding, position, evaluator,
  signal. Technical-philosophical hybrid, plain and precise, no buzzword inflation.
- **Signature phrases (his own, reuse in his posts):** "the human stays in the loop
  on purpose", "without becoming a rubber stamp", "nothing ships without my
  judgment", "AI amplifies, it does not replace", comprehension before shipping.
- **Mechanics:** comma for connecting phrases, never em dash; never space-comma-space.
- **Do:** open on a strategic observation; take a position early; let the principle
  surface in his own words; one well-placed informal pivot allowed ("a structured
  roast") amid professional prose.
- **Don't:** narrative scene-setting; hype; em dashes; AI-tell patterns (rule-of-three
  inflation, "it's not just X, it's Y" overuse).

## Action for blog-poster

- Adopt this as the default voice for the author's blog posts.
- Pass it as the runtime register context (DSM_0.2 §23.4) to the humanizer and any
  drafting subagent.
- Canonical example: the in-progress "Model-as-Reader: Auditing Human-AI
  Collaboration Docs With Fable 5" post (DSM Central, S222).
