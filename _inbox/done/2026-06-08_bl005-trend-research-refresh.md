## Headline finding

The human engineer is back at the center, with the AI used for leverage and speed rather than as a substitute for judgment. The sharpest signal is a reversal by the automation camp itself (Horthy and HumanLayer: "read and own the code. No exceptions."), echoed by vendors who would profit from full autonomy (Anthropic, "you become the verification loop"; GitHub, "judgment is the bottleneck"; Cognition, "human review is still necessary").

Role chronology: author (Copilot 2021), to director (vibe coding, Feb 2025), to context architect (mid 2025), to architect and owner (harness engineering plus read-and-own-the-code, 2026).
